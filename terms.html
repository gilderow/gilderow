ReachForge for Gilderow · Effective 15 November 2026

1. What ReachForge is

ReachForge is a private software tool of Gilderow, an Etsy shop selling digital wall art. It runs on the shop owner's own computer and prepares and sends Gilderow's own artwork to Gilderow's own accounts on Instagram, Pinterest and TikTok. Every post is reviewed and approved by the owner. For TikTok, videos are sent to the owner's TikTok inbox and the owner finishes and publishes each post in the TikTok app.

2. Who may use it

ReachForge is used only by the owner of Gilderow for Gilderow's own accounts. It is not offered, sold or made available to other people, and it has no public sign-up.

3. Content

All content sent through ReachForge is Gilderow's own artwork and text. AI-assisted artwork is disclosed as such on every post (by the platform's AI label where available, otherwise in the caption). The owner remains responsible for each post and follows the rules of each platform, including the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

How data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: data stays on the owner's computer, is used only to publish Gilderow's own content, and is not shared or sold.
