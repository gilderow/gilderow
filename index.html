ReachForge

ReachForge is Gilderow's private publishing tool. It runs on the shop owner's computer and shares Gilderow's own artwork on Gilderow's own Instagram and Pinterest accounts. Every post is approved by the owner before it goes out, links to its Etsy listing, and is labelled as AI-assisted digital art.

It is not a service for other people and collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.
